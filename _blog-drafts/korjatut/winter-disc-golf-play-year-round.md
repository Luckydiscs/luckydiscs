Finland has long winters, but that doesn't mean your season has to end in October. Snow quietens the course, the fairways are peaceful, and a clean release in crisp air feels especially rewarding. With the right gear and a few simple tricks you can keep playing comfortably through the winter.

## Dress warm and in layers

A winter round is long, and you stand still between throws. Wear several thin layers so you can adjust to moving and resting. Use thin gloves that still give you a solid grip on the disc. Bulky mittens ruin your release.

Keep your hands and discs warm between throws. Warm plastic and a warm grip feel completely different from frozen ones. A small flask and a hand warmer make the whole round far more enjoyable.

## Find your disc in the snow

Snow swallows a disc fast. Choose brightly coloured discs: red, orange or yellow stands out against white ground far better than a dark colour.

Tie a length of ribbon to the disc so it stays visible even in deep snow. That small fluttering tail saves a lot of digging and keeps the round moving. Always fix in your mind exactly where the disc disappeared before you walk over to look.

## Discs fly differently in the cold

In the cold the plastic stiffens and grip matters more than ever. Discs fly more stable than usual in frost and often finish shorter than in summer. A slightly more understable or lighter disc compensates for this and brings back the turn the disc would have in summer.

Durable plastic, like the Ultrium in the Jailbreak, copes with hard hits against icy trees and rocks. The Money Shot is for putting and approaches, the Bank Robber gives controlled distance, and the Treasure Hunt goes furthest when you need maximum distance off the tee.

## Plan your round for daylight

Winter days are short, so start your round early. Allow plenty of time, since walking through snow is slower. Pack a head torch just in case.

Keep it clean too: take your rubbish with you so the course is in good shape in spring when the snow melts.

## Winter is a season of its own

Winter disc golf at its best is calm, beautiful and surprisingly addictive. With the right gear and durable discs the game carries on even when the ground is white.
