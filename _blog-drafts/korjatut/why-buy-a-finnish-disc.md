You can order a disc golf disc from anywhere in the world. A Finnish-made disc still has practical advantages: delivery is quick, there are no surprise charges, and customer service is close to home. Lucky Discs are made in Nokia, Finland.

## Made in Nokia, Finland

Lucky Discs are made in Nokia from premium European plastics. With production close by, quality is easier to keep an eye on and batches stay consistent. Durable plastic holds its flight characteristics for a long time, even when the disc hits trees and rocks.

## Fast delivery, no customs fees within the EU

Ordering from outside the EU can mean long delivery times, customs processing and unexpected extra charges. Within the EU there are no customs fees, and from a domestic shop your package arrives quickly: we ship orders within 1–3 business days via Posti or Matkahuolto, and you receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves. Shipping costs 5.90 EUR and is free on orders over 50 EUR. We currently ship within Finland only.

## Your money stays in Finland

When you buy a Finnish disc, your money goes to a Finnish company and supports Finnish work. Lucky Discs is a small company, so every order makes a direct difference to us.

## Customer service close to home

The difference shows after the purchase too. You get service in Finnish, returns and complaints are handled close to home, and you have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal. If anything is on your mind, reach us at asiakaspalvelu@luckydiscs.fi.

## Discs for different throws

The range has four discs: the Money Shot (4 / 3 / 1 / 3) is a putt-and-approach disc for the shots near the basket, the Jailbreak (5 / 4 / 0 / 2) a straight-flying midrange for all-round play, the Bank Robber (8 / 5 / -1 / 2) a fairway driver for controlled distance and the Treasure Hunt (12 / 6 / -1 / 3) a distance driver for the longest throws.
