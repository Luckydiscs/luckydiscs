Disc golf is an established pastime in Finland. According to the Finnish Disc Golf Association, around 228,000 adults play the sport and roughly 60,000 of them play every week. For a retailer, that is a large and steady customer base that needs discs every season.

## What the numbers say

Disc golf is not a fringe sport. According to the sports participation study by KIHU (the Finnish Research Institute for Olympic Sports), disc golf is the 12th most popular sport among Finnish adults, and among men it ranks seventh.

The number of players has not grown in recent years, though. The association's figures put players at 263,000 in 2018 and around 228,000 now. Its membership has fallen from 11,869 (2023) to 10,527 (2025). The honest picture is a large, established player base, not accelerating growth.

People still play a lot. In 2025 the Metrix scoring service recorded 11,215 weekly league events with 147,794 player entries. Finland has more than 900 courses (UDisc 2022: 988).

Wider interest was measured in a Sponsor Navigator survey in 2021, which found that around 700,000 Finnish adults were interested in disc golf.

## What does this mean for retailers?

A mature sport means steady demand. Discs get lost in the woods and in lakes, plastic wears out, and players top up their bags year after year. New players keep joining even though the total is not rising. Competition in retail is tough all the same, so the shops that do well are those with good value for money, quick availability and a supplier that supports their sales.
