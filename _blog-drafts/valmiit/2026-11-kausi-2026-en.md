2026 was an exceptional season for Finnish disc golfers. Finnish players won a world title and several of the sport's most prestigious events, the majors, and at home the national titles were decided in Joensuu.

Here are the highlights of the season. The source for every result is listed at the end. Information is correct as of 4 November 2026.

## Eveliina Salonen is world champion

The biggest news of the season came from the United States at the end of August. Eveliina Salonen won the PDGA Pro World Championships in Milford on 26–30 August 2026, by a margin of 17 strokes. Iida Lehtomäki finished eighth in the same event.

In June, Salonen had already won the European Open in Tallinn by four strokes. With that win she has now won all four majors in her career.

## Finnish wins in the United States

In April, at the Champions Cup in Virginia, both main divisions went to Finland: Niklas Anttila won the open division and Silva Saarinen the women's division.

The same month, Henna Blomroos won the Jonesboro Open, her first event win on the Disc Golf Pro Tour, the sport's leading professional tour. She won by one stroke.

In July, Silva Saarinen won the United States Women's Disc Golf Championship, another major. According to MTV Uutiset, she was ranked number one in the world in August, and by then a Finnish woman had won all three majors played that season.

## The Heinola Open brought the tour to Finland

The Disc Golf Pro Tour's Heinola Open was played at Kippasuo on 10–12 July 2026. This time the wins went abroad: Gannon Buhr won the open division and Anniken Kristiansen Steen the women's division. The best Finn was Daniel Davidsson in a tie for third. Onni Ruusunen and Justus Sarvi tied for sixth.

## National championships: titles for Arkko and Kindstedt

The Finnish Disc Golf Championships were played at Lykynlampi in Joensuu on 23–26 July 2026.

Nikola Arkko won the open division at -23. Defending champion Daniel Davidsson was second (-21) and Niklas Anttila third (-19).

Olivia Kindstedt won the women's title at +6, the first national title of her career. Asta Hapulahti was second (+16) and Netta Leppäaho third (+18).

Daniel Davidsson is a familiar name to us: he won the 2025 Finnish Championship representing Lucky Discs. In 2026 he played for another manufacturer's team.

## What this says about Finnish disc golf

Finnish players are no longer just taking part in the world's top events. They are winning them, especially in the women's division. At the same time, the defending national champion finished second, which shows how deep the Finnish field at the top has become.
